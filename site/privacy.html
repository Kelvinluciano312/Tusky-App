Tusky Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

Tusky is a personal finance app made by Ouroboros Studios. This page explains what Tusky collects, why, who else handles it, and how to delete it. We do not sell your data, and Tusky shows no ads.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and the display name you choose.
  • Bank data, when you connect a bank: account names, the last digits of account numbers, balances, and transactions (date, amount, merchant, the description your bank sends, and a category). Your bank login never reaches us: you sign in through Plaid, and Tusky's servers keep only Plaid's access token, which the app itself never sees.
  • What you add: categories, budgets, notes, merchant rules, who paid, splits, and settle-up payments.
  • Your plan: which plan you have, its status and expiry date. Payment details stay with Google Play or the App Store; we never see your card.

Who sees it

  • You.
  • Your herd. If you invite people into a shared herd, they see the herd's accounts and transactions, except accounts you mark private.
  • No one else at Tusky looks at your data, except to fix a problem you report to us.

Services that process data for us

  • Supabase: hosts our database and servers.
  • Plaid: connects your bank and delivers your bank data (Plaid's privacy policy).
  • RevenueCat, Google Play and the App Store: handle subscriptions. RevenueCat knows your Tusky user ID and what you bought, not your bank data.
  • TypeSafe AI, only if you turn on "Let AI help sort and review": receives a transaction's merchant, amount, bank description and category, to suggest a category or flag it for review. It never receives your name, email, balances or account details. This is off until you turn it on.

Shared category fixes (optional)

If you turn on "Share my fixes, anonymously", the merchant, the category you chose, and a rough amount range join a pool that helps every Tusky user. The pool holds no name, email, account or exact amount; each contribution is keyed by a one-way code, so we can delete yours but not trace it back to you. A category is served from the pool only when at least three people agree. Turning the switch off deletes everything you shared.

On your device

Your sign-in is stored in your phone's secure keystore. Tusky uses no advertising or tracking SDKs.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you disconnect a bank, you choose whether to keep its history or delete it. When you delete your account, everything is deleted.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete account. This disconnects every bank at Plaid and deletes your account and data. If you share a herd, the herd keeps what belongs to it (such as budgets and categories), and your banks and transactions leave with you and are deleted. More on the account deletion page.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play or the App Store.

Children

Tusky is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above. For a significant change, we tell you in the app first.
