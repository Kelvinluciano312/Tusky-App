Delete your Tusky account

Tusky, by Ouroboros Studios

In the app

  1. Open Tusky and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account and confirm.

If you pay for a plan through Google Play or the App Store, Tusky reminds you to cancel it there first: deleting your account does not stop the store from charging you.

Can't sign in?

Email ouroboros2043@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Tusky account". We delete it within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Every connected bank is disconnected at Plaid first, so Plaid stops sharing data with us.
  • Your account, email, display name, and plan record.
  • Your accounts, balances, transactions, notes, budgets, categories and rules.
  • Any category fixes you shared anonymously.
  • Your record at RevenueCat, our subscription service.

What stays

  • A shared herd keeps what belongs to the herd, such as its budgets, categories and rules. Your banks and transactions leave with you and are deleted. Settle-up payments recorded between you and other members are deleted too.
  • Store purchase records stay with Google or Apple, under their policies.

Nothing else is kept. Deletion cannot be undone.
